Timberhouse was not designed and dropped onto the hill — it was cut into it. The foundation follows the original grade of the slope; the timber came from trees felled to clear the footprint.

Built 1962 · Restored 2019 9 Rooms · 3 Floors Architect: Espen Solberg

Floor 00 — Ground

Arrival & the hearth

You enter through a low timber door into a single room that runs the width of the house: stone hearth on one end, kitchen on the other, and a table long enough for everyone staying that week to eat at once.

Floor 01 — The Suites

Where the rooms are

Six rooms sit along a single corridor lit by a skylight cut the length of the roofline. Every room keeps the original beam work, and every window faces the tree line rather than the driveway.

6 roomsSkylight corridorOriginal beams
Floor 02 — The Terrace

The part with the view

The roof was flattened during the 2019 restoration to hold a cedar-lined plunge pool and a dinner table for twelve. On clear nights you can see the lights of the valley floor, forty minutes below.

Plunge poolOutdoor tableValley view

Nine rooms, no two alike

Each room was fitted around what was already there — a chimney, a low ceiling, a window that couldn't be moved — rather than to a repeatable floor plan.
